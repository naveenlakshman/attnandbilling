How to Import Excel or CSV Data
  1. Download Template: Download the sample Excel template (.xlsx) for your desired entity. Open it to use built-in dropdown selections for your institute's branches and common fields.
  2. Branch Name Selection: Click any cell in the branch_name column to choose from a pre-filled dropdown list of your institute's active branches (e.g. Sulibele, Main Branch).
  3. Automatic Student Code: Leave the student_code column blank if you want the system to automatically generate student registration codes based on your Company Profile Prefix.
  4. Select & Upload: Choose the target table from the dropdown above, select your Excel or CSV file, and click Import File.
  5. Check Results: View success and validation error messages upon completion.
⚠️ Multi-Tenant & Data Safety Rules:
  • Automatic Institute Scoping: All imported records are automatically isolated and assigned to your current active institute.
  • Referential Integrity: ID columns (such as branch_id, student_id, invoice_id, lead_id, user_id) must exist and belong to your active institute.
  • Date Format: Use DD-MM-YYYY format (e.g., 21-03-2026) or YYYY-MM-DD.
  • Numbers & Currency: Use raw numbers without currency symbols (e.g. 5000, not ₹5,000).
  • Plan Limits: Bulk imports respect active student, branch, and staff user limits for your institute's subscription plan.
  • CSV Export Tips:
    • Save Excel/Sheets as "CSV UTF-8 (.csv)" format.
    • Multi-line text in cells must be enclosed in quotes.
    • Avoid trailing spaces in column headers.
